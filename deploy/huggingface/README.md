# Alpine

Practice a job interview out loud with an AI interviewer, get scores that quote what you said,
and a next interview aimed at your weakest answers.

This Space runs on a free CPU: speech recognition uses Whisper small, and saved sessions reset when
the Space restarts. Each browser only sees its own sessions. Scores are practice guidance from an AI,
not a hiring decision.

Source: https://github.com/Owsper/Alpine
